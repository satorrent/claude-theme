## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-09

First release.

### Added

- Six preset themes for claude.ai, listed from lightest to darkest: Cream & green, Cream & terracotta, Cream & navy, Navy & orange, Black & orange and Black & green.
- Visible borders on the sidebar, header, your message bubbles, the message box, menus and code blocks.
- Live preview from the popup on the open claude.ai tab, with an Apply button and a way back to Claude's original look.
- The chosen theme is injected as the page starts loading.
- Contrast audit at build time: every theme must meet WCAG AA (4.5:1 for text, 3:1 for borders and controls). The popup shows each theme's text contrast.
- One-click tab reload when a tab was opened before the extension was installed or updated.
- English and Spanish interface.
